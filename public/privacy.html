Delivering the site

Every request goes through Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA), which delivers the site and protects it from attacks. Cloudflare processes your IP address and the details of the request on my behalf under a data processing agreement. Data may be processed in the USA; Cloudflare is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. The game server itself runs in Germany and keeps no access log.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, my interest in delivering the site securely and reliably.

Playing

To play, you enter a name. Your name, the matches you create or join, your moves and the chat messages you send go to the game server so the other players in your match can see them. Match names and the player names in open matches are shown in the lobby to every visitor. All of this is kept only in the server's memory and is never written to disk. If you leave or stay away for a while, you are taken out of the match, and a match nobody is connected to any more is deleted after a few minutes. Everything is also gone when the server restarts. Please do not use your real name if you do not want others to see it.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR, because this is what you ask for when you play. Giving a name is voluntary, but without one you cannot join a match.

Stored in your browser

The site saves the name you entered in your browser's local storage so you do not have to type it again. For each match you join it also keeps your seat and the key for it, so reloading the page takes you back to your seat; this is removed when you leave the match. It stays on your device and you can remove it by clearing the site data in your browser. This is necessary for the function you asked for (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG).

There are no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no content from third parties. Fonts and images are served by this site itself. Links to GitHub and explodingkittens.com only send you there when you click them.
